Frequently asked questions

Is EzyCommunity really 0% transaction fee?

EzyCommunity states that it charges 0% platform transaction fees. Payment processor fees, such as Stripe fees, are still separate and apply according to the processor's terms.

Is the branded mobile app included?

No. The branded iOS and Android app is currently offered as a paid add-on. The official pricing page lists $199/month, $149/month billed yearly, or $3,490 one-time.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

The official pricing page currently states that the 14-day free trial does not require a card.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Custom domain and white-label branding are included on the current Pro and Business plans.

Is email automation available?

The official site currently marks Email Campaigns, Automation and EzyAI as "Coming soon", so we do not present them as fully available features.
