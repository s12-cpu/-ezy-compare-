FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really 0% transaction fees? +

EzyCommunity states that it charges 0% platform transaction fees. Standard payment processor fees, such as Stripe fees, still apply.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes. The current official pricing page states that all plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Do I get a branded mobile app? +

A branded iOS and Android app is available as a paid add-on. It is not included in every standard plan.

Can I use my own domain? +

Yes. Custom domains and white-label branding are included in the current paid plans.

Can I cancel later? +

The official pricing page states that you can upgrade or downgrade from your dashboard.
