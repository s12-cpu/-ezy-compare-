Frequently Asked Questions

Does EzyCommunity really charge 0%? +

EzyCommunity states that it charges 0% platform transaction fees on its paid plans. This does not mean payment processing is free. Stripe or another payment processor may still charge its normal fees.

Is the branded mobile app included for free? +

No. The branded iOS and Android app is a paid add-on. The exact price depends on the current offer and billing option.

Is there a free trial? +

The official pricing page currently advertises a 14-day free trial with no card required.

Can I use my own domain? +

Yes. Custom domains and white-label branding are listed among EzyCommunity's platform features.
